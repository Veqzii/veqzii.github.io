How to play
  1. Spin for £2. You start with £200 of pretend money and can top up any time.
  2. Match symbols. 3, 4 or 5 of the same everyday symbol in a straight line (across, down or diagonal) win £1, £3 or £6. The winning line glows.
  3. Go for the big prizes. 8 or more 🔮 gives 2 free spins. 12 or more 👑 or 🗝️ is a jackpot.
  4. Watch the pity meter. Each spin without a jackpot makes the next one more likely, and spin 90 is guaranteed. Winning a jackpot resets the meter.
Jackpot (5★)
👑 pays £80 and 🗝️ pays £26. Your chance starts at 0.6% per spin, climbs fast from spin 74, and reaches 100% at spin 90.
The 50/50
Half of all jackpots are the featured 👑. If you get 🗝️ instead, your next jackpot is guaranteed to be 👑.
Free spins (4★)
8+ 🔮 gives 2 spins at no cost. They still build your pity, and line wins pay ×1 then ×2. You get at least one of these every 10 spins.
Cash out or carry
After winning a 👑 you choose. Cash out to take the money. Carry to double or nothing: your pity jumps to 73, and your next jackpot doubles the pot if it’s 👑 or loses it if it’s 🗝️. You can carry again after a win.
Safety net
When you carry, pay 25% of the pot to keep half of it if you lose. It makes the risk smaller without changing your average result.
Pity Boost
Pay £12 to add 10 to your pity, a shortcut past about 5 spins.
Gravity mode
Winning symbols burst and new ones fall in. Switch the toggle off to keep the board still.
Board events
Sometimes the board mirrors or flashes with lightning. It’s just for show and never changes your odds.
Auto-spin and Turbo
Auto-spin keeps going until you stop it, run out of money, or win a 👑 that needs your choice. Turbo speeds up the animations.
Payback
Around 94% over many spins if you always cash out.
